## What it is

A personal portfolio that treats the web like film stock — every image carries **35mm perforations**, the layout breathes in a **bento grid**, and transitions feel like cuts between scenes.

Built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks. No build step. Just the browser, the DOM, and a lot of attention to detail.

---

## What it does

### 🎞️ Film Perforations
Every image and video is framed with procedurally generated 35mm sprocket holes. The perforations scale with aspect ratio — 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16 — and render as a transparent overlay, not a border. It looks like film because it *is* film logic.

### 🍱 Bento Grid Gallery
Projects flow in a responsive bento grid with real aspect ratios preserved. No forced cropping. No preset frames. Drag and drop to reorder — your arrangement persists locally.

### ✨ Smooth Motion
- **Layout shuffles** when filtering projects — cards don't just appear, they travel to their new positions
- **Scroll-triggered reveals** that feel cinematic, not corporate
- **Micro-interactions** on every hover, every click, every state change
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support — animations never block access

### 🌗 Dark & Light Mode
Respects `prefers-color-scheme` out of the box. Warm bronze accent (`#c4956a`) against soft surfaces. No jarring contrast, no pure black voids.

---

## Design Philosophy

> *"Forma následuje funkci."*

But form must have a soul. This site rejects the polished-corporate-default. It favors **analog textures translated into digital interfaces** — film grain, perforation mechanics, the rhythm of a cut. Typography pairs a grotesque sans (Bricolage Grotesque) with a sharp serif (Cormorant Garamond) for tension between utility and elegance.
